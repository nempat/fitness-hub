QUESTIONS

GOOD TO KNOW.

How do I activate my membership?

Pay by UPI, submit the 12-digit UTR in your account, and the gym owner will approve it after verifying the payment.

How long does approval take?

Approval is done manually by the gym owner, so it may take some time. Your request shows as pending until then.

What if my payment is rejected?

You will see the reason in your payments list and can submit a new request with the correct UTR.

Can I renew early?

Yes. If you renew while your plan is active, the new plan starts when the current one ends.
